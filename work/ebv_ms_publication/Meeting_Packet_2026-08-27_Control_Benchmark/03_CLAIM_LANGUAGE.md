# Claim language for the call

## Safe to say

- "The candidate composite recovered the established positive in the top 3 in all eight completed held-out panels."
- "Each biological TCR system was excluded from the data used to select its test-fold weights."
- "The worst completed-panel rank improved from 9 under exposed C-alpha RMSD alone to 3 under the composite."
- "No completed composite panel failed the top-3 rule."
- "The formal trust gate remains not evaluable, so the weights are provisional and discovery rankings remain frozen."
- "TCR-facing sequence identity performed at least as well, so independent value from predicted 3D geometry has not been demonstrated."

## Do not say

- "The model is fully validated."
- "The discovery candidates are control-validated."
- "AlphaFold predicts TCR cross-reactivity."
- "The score is a probability, confidence of mimicry, or false-discovery rate."
- "The controls prove antigen presentation, T-cell activation, molecular mimicry, or an MS mechanism."
- "The missing panels passed based on their available ranks."

## If asked, "What exactly is validated?"

> The completed-panel recovery behavior is validated against held-out established controls: eight of eight completed panels place the positive in the top 3. The full benchmark and the discovery model are not formally validated because four required evaluations remain incomplete and the sequence-only baseline performs as well or better.

## If asked, "Can we rerank the EBV-self candidates now?"

> Not under the predeclared trust rule. The formal gate is not evaluable, the weights remain unfrozen, and the earlier discovery rankings have deliberately not been changed.

## If asked, "Why does sequence identity matter?"

> A structural model should ideally add useful information beyond simply noticing similar exposed amino acids. Here, TCR-facing sequence identity recovered all eight controls with a worst rank of 2, while the composite's worst rank was 3. That means control recovery alone is not yet evidence that the 3D component is contributing independent signal.
