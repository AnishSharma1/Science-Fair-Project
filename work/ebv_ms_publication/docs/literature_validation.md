# EBV evidence validation

The EBV rows trace to eight PubMed-indexed publications. Their titles and
dates were rechecked through NCBI PubMed on 2026-08-01.

| PMID | What it contributes | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| [39432795](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39432795/) | DRB1*15:01-restricted CD4 T cells recognizing EBV glycoproteins | Direct primary evidence |
| [33091337](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33091337/) | HLA-DR15 autoreactive repertoire in MS | Direct disease/HLA context |
| [7534214](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7534214/) | Foundational viral-peptide / MBP molecular-mimicry study | Mechanistic precedent, not an EBV-DRB1*15:01 validation by itself |
| [17471168](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17471168/) | HLA-II EBV peptide cocktail | EBV class-II evidence; allele-specific interpretation required |
| [20019813](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20019813/) | EBV antigen sources for CD4/CD8 epitope display | Antigen-processing context |
| [34882759](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34882759/) | EBV capsid-specific CD4 responses | T-cell evidence; not necessarily DRB1*15:01-specific |
| [31736946](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31736946/) | EBV-specific T-cell expansion after donor lymphocyte infusion | Contextual EBV response evidence |
| [17559580](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17559580/) | Cross-reactive autoimmune TCR recognition across HLA-II molecules | Structural/immunological context |

The primary analysis should give greatest weight to PMID 39432795 because its
title directly matches the locked allele/class scope. The other papers are
supporting or contextual evidence and must not be pooled as if they were
replicate experiments.
