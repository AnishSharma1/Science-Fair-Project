# Revised claim and validation language

## Abstract / results-safe wording

We developed a reproducible HLA class-II pMHC candidate-prioritization workflow for EBV and CNS/myelin peptides. The workflow ranks predeclared pairs using local sequence, physicochemical, and modeled peptide-backbone features after HLA-groove fitting. Experimental BALF5--MBP pMHC structures serve as a literature-grounded structural calibration anchor. The resulting rankings are hypothesis-generating and do not demonstrate equivalent HLA-II binding registers, common TCR recognition, T-cell activation, or multiple-sclerosis mechanism.

## Correct language for the BALF5--MBP system

The BALF5--MBP system is used as **positive-control calibration** and a **literature-overlay anchor**. It is not described as fully independent external validation because multiple positive records overlap the same established biological system. Its allelic context must remain explicit: BALF5 is presented by DRB5*01:01 (DR2a) and MBP by DRB1*15:01 (DR2b), both on the DR15 haplotype.

## Correct language for the current external overlay

The strict new-literature overlay is exploratory and non-confirmatory: 2 of 5 positives appear in the top 10 of the 32-pair universe, with empirical p = 0.5036. This result should be reported as an informative limitation, not omitted or recast as validation.

## What a future register-aware analysis could say

If predeclared plausible registers and matched decoys support discrimination, the strongest permitted conclusion is:

> Register-aware pMHC features improved prioritization of selected literature-supported/control pairs relative to matched decoys under the stated computational model.

It must still not be paraphrased as evidence that a TCR binds both ligands, that either peptide is cross-reactive, or that either pair contributes to MS.

## Current register-aware result-safe wording

The pre-meeting register diagnostic found that most locally aligned residues in the 32-pair screen do not occupy the same P1--P9 position under the current IEDB top-core hypotheses. This finding is a method-design constraint: it motivates a pre-specified register-aware redesign and must not be recast as a biological null result or resolved by selecting favorable alternative windows post hoc.

## Phrases to avoid

- "proves molecular mimicry"
- "predicts TCR cross-reactivity"
- "demonstrates shared TCR recognition"
- "validates an EBV-driven MS mechanism"
- "independent external validation" for BALF5--MBP overlap records
