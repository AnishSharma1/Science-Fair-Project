# Hy.2E11 sequence audit

## Verified primary-source information

The primary literature identifies the Hy.2E11 receptor as using human
V-alpha 3.1 and V-beta 4/4.3 variable regions. It reports the junctional
amino-acid segments as:

- alpha: `TDSGGSYIPTFGRGTSLIVHP`
- beta: `PSGQGTYGYTFGSGTRLTVV`

The 2002 cross-reactivity paper states that rearranged human V-alpha 3.1 and
V-beta 4.3 variable regions were PCR amplified from genomic DNA to make the
transfectant and transgenic-mouse constructs.

## Decision

Do **not** construct a full Hy.2E11 alpha/beta protein sequence from these
annotations. The reports do not give the exact V-gene allele sequence,
complete rearranged nucleotide sequence, leader/constant-region design, or a
machine-readable sequence record. Choosing a reference germline allele would
be an inference and would compromise the provenance of a ternary prediction.

## Allowed uses now

- Cite Hy.2E11 as the established experimental positive-control receptor.
- Use PDB 1H15 and 1BX2 as the two experimentally determined pMHC ligands.
- Use the reported V-gene and CDR3-junction annotations in a methods/literature
  table, explicitly marked as partial receptor information.

## Required before a Hy.2E11 ternary prediction

Obtain a complete alpha and beta sequence directly from the original construct,
the authors, or a traceable sequence record that provides every residue.
