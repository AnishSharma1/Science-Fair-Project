# Results: experimental calibration of the BALF5--MBP positive control

We first established the biological and structural ground truth for the
EBV--myelin comparison. The previously reported Hy.2E11 cross-reactive system
recognizes MBP(85--99) when presented by HLA-DRB1*15:01 (DR2b) and the EBV
BALF5(627--641) peptide when presented by HLA-DRB5*01:01 (DR2a). Thus, this
system involves two distinct class-II molecules encoded on the DR15 haplotype;
it is not a same-allele DRB1*15:01 comparison.

We used the corresponding experimental pMHC structures (PDB 1BX2 and 1H15)
as a positive control. After fitting the peptide-binding platforms of the HLA
molecules, the seven-position BALF5/MBP peptide core had a C-alpha RMSD of
0.838 Å. This result documents structural equivalence of the experimentally
observed pMHC surfaces. It does not independently measure Hy.2E11 affinity,
activation, or cross-reactivity; those functions were established by the prior
experimental literature.

The 86-model ColabFold pMHC screen is therefore interpreted as an exploratory
candidate-prioritization analysis, calibrated against this known structural
positive control. No ternary-model score is treated as evidence of selective
TCR recognition because the Ob.1A12 calibration did not recover its known
experimental pose.
